# Product Vision — RAWAQA

## Product Promise

Every RAWAQA seat is designed around real moments of rest. The website should feel as calm and intentional as the products: easy to browse, confident to buy, reliable after purchase.

## Audience

Egyptian consumers seeking premium bean bags for home, gaming, kids, and outdoor use — primarily mobile-first shoppers in EGP.

## Differentiation

- Premium editorial design (not marketplace generic)  
- Lifestyle categories: Relax, Game, Kids, Outdoor  
- Local: EGP, Egypt delivery, COD  
- Operations: Odoo + SMS integrated  

## Business Outcomes

- Increase online revenue channel  
- Reduce manual order entry  
- Professional brand presence  
- Scalable catalog management  

## Experience Principles

1. Clarity over clutter  
2. Trust at checkout  
3. Mobile-first  
4. Honest availability  
5. Post-purchase confidence (track + SMS)  

## Long-Term Direction

- Payment gateway integration  
- Full Arabic i18n  
- Inventory sync from Odoo  
- Customer reviews and loyalty
