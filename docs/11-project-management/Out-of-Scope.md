# Out of Scope — RAWAQA

The following items are **explicitly excluded** from the 35,000 EGP agreement. Any request requires a formal **Change Request** with impact assessment and client approval before work begins.

---

## Features & Pages

- New pages not in agreed sitemap  
- Major redesign or rebrand after approval  
- Additional customer-facing features (wishlist, coupons, loyalty, subscriptions)  
- Blog / CMS content management  
- Live chat integration  
- Product recommendation engine / AI features  

---

## Technical Scope

- Additional REST APIs beyond documented contract  
- Mobile native applications (iOS/Android)  
- Additional third-party integrations (payment gateways, analytics, Meta, Google Ads)  
- Webhook receivers for external systems (unless CR)  
- Multi-warehouse / multi-vendor marketplace  
- Advanced search (Elasticsearch) unless CR  

---

## Odoo — Out of Scope

- Accounting module integration  
- Automatic invoicing  
- POS synchronization  
- Full bidirectional inventory sync  
- Payment reconciliation  
- Advanced shipping module setup  
- Manufacturing / MRP modules  
- Full ERP synchronization  
- Custom Odoo module development  

*See [Odoo Integration Specification](../06-integrations/Odoo-Integration-Specification.md) for in-scope boundary.*

---

## SMS — Out of Scope

- Unlimited SMS usage (client pays per message)  
- WhatsApp Business integration  
- Marketing / promotional SMS campaigns  
- OTP / two-factor authentication (unless CR)  
- International SMS outside Egypt  

---

## Content & Creative

- Professional product photography  
- Video production  
- Copywriting and translation services  
- Manual product data entry (bulk)  
- Logo or brand identity design (existing brand used)  

---

## Operations & Fees

- Hosting fees  
- Domain registration and renewal  
- SMS usage fees and provider subscription  
- WhatsApp usage fees  
- SSL certificate (if paid)  
- Odoo hosting/licensing fees  
- Payment gateway transaction fees  
- Ongoing maintenance after handover period  
- 24/7 support SLA (unless separate agreement)  

---

## Process

All out-of-scope requests → [Change Request System](Change-Request-System.md)
