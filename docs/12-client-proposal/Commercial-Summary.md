| **Platform** | Web (not mobile app) |

---

## What 35,000 EGP Delivers

A **complete custom software platform**:

1. **Customer storefront** — Browse, configure, purchase, track  
2. **Backend & database** — Secure API, orders, customers, products  
3. **Admin dashboard** — Manage catalog and fulfillment  
4. **Odoo integration** — Orders flow automatically to ERP  
5. **SMS notifications** — Customers receive order confirmation  
6. **Testing & launch** — Staging, QA, production deployment  

This is **engineering and integration work**, not a template or design-only deliverable.
